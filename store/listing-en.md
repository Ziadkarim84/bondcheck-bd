# Play Store listing — English (en-US)

**App name (30):** BondCheck BD: Prize Bond Check

**Short description (80):** Find out automatically if your Bangladesh prize bonds won. No account needed.

**Full description:**
Did any of your prize bonds ever win? BondCheck BD checks your bond numbers against every official Bangladesh Bank draw and tells you the moment one of them wins.

Why BondCheck BD
• No account, password or OTP — open it and start
• Your bond numbers stay on your phone
• Checks every draw of the last two years (the claim window) at once
• Add bonds one by one or as a range (e.g. 0123400–0123499)
• A notification when a new draw is published
• Prize amount after 20% tax and the claim deadline, all in one place
• Quick check for any number
• Bangla and English, light and dark mode
• Move your list to a new phone with a backup file

The free version holds 30 bonds; watch a short ad for 3 more slots at a time (up to 60). Get Pro once for unlimited bonds and no ads — no subscription.

Results come from Bangladesh Bank (bb.org.bd). Always confirm with the official result before claiming. This app is not affiliated with Bangladesh Bank or the government.
